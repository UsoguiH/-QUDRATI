## The five, and the idea behind them

| # | State | Where he lives | Why people will love him there |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | `teach` | «قبل ما نبدأ» title card of the teaching cards, the old lesson intro, the «كيف أحلّها؟» method sheet | He is a teacher. This is the pose that says so, and the teaching cards are his classroom. |
| 7 | `wait` | The «تبي توقف الدرس؟» dialog, and the mock's «تبي توقف المحاكاة؟» | Duolingo's biggest character moment: Duo asks you to stay. Ours is worried for you, never guilt. |
| 8 | `read` | Lesson path, once today's lesson is banked | Your own Duolingo screenshot: Duo sits on a stack of books, reading. After you finish, he studies too. |
| 9 | `sleep` | Lesson path, banked and late at night | Duolingo's most loved state, inverted: he sleeps because your work is done, not because you skipped it. |
| 10 | `crown` | Streak milestones at 7 / 30 / 100 days, the champion rank, the top of the league | Milestones are what people screenshot and share. A crowned teacher is the picture they keep. |

With the first five, the path now has a routine, and that is what makes a character feel
alive rather than pasted on:

| Time and state | Pose | Motion |
|---|---|---|
| Default | `stand` | slow `bob` |
| Evening, streak alive, today not banked | `concerned` | `bob-fast` |
| Today's lesson banked | `read` | slow `bob` |
| Banked and 22:00 or later | `sleep` | none |
| Exam within 7 days | `strong` | slow `bob` |

## Wiring notes per placement

Symbols, not line numbers.

| State | Function | What to do | Motion |
|---|---|---|---|
| `teach` | `renderTeach()` title step, `renderLessonIntro()`, `A.showMethod()` | Hero of the «قبل ما نبدأ» card and the old intro head. On the method sheet he sits at the top: the sheet already calls `stopQTimer()`, so it is reading time, not clock time. The coach line keeps its bulb. | `pop` |
| `wait` | `askConfirm()` | The dialog has no hero slot. Give it one the way `showModal()` has, then pass `mascot("wait","pop")` from `A.quitSession` and `A.quitMock`. He stands above the title, the «أكمل الدرس» button stays the primary. | `pop` |
| `read` | `renderPath()` | When `S.streak.last === todayKey()` (today's lesson is banked), render `read` in the `path-mascot` slot instead of `stand`. | slow `bob` |
| `sleep` | `renderPath()` | Same condition as `read` plus `new Date().getHours() >= 22`. Draw two or three small «z» in CSS above his head, fading up on a loop, so they disappear with `data-motion="reduced"` like every other animation. | none |
| `crown` | `showStreakCelebration()`, `showRankUp()`, `renderLeague()` | Replace `celebrate` when `count` is 7, 30 or 100. Hero of the rank-up ceremony at the champion tier. On the league screen, beside the `lb-prog-max` line only. | `pop` |

Never twice on one screen. `read` and `sleep` replace `stand`, they never join it.
