# Rule study: five ways to teach the same two rules

*2026-10-27. Five prototype lessons in `tools/rulestudy/`, each teaching the same two rules, «العدد × مرّاته» (repeated addition is multiplication) and «حيلة الطرفين» (1 + … + n = n × (n + 1) ÷ 2). Fifteen simulated Qudrat students played all five, in a shuffled order. They were not told what was being compared, and the lessons carried colour codes only.*

| Code | Method | Idea |
|---|---|---|
| **sand** | A «خمّن ثم اكتشف» | Guess first, then tap to discover the trick, then build the rule before it is ever stated |
| **rose** | B «من الحبّات للقاعدة» | Concrete → picture → symbols → words: tap blocks into a grid or staircase, label a diagram, then write the rule |
| **sky** | C «أمثلة تتلاشى» | A 10-second skip challenge, then a worked example, one step missing, solve alone, then name the rule |
| **lime** | D «اسمها وليش» | Give the rule a sticker name, ask «ليش تشتغل؟», apply it by name, rebuild it from the name |
| **sun** | E «أيّ قاعدة؟» | Teach each rule fast, then train discrimination: sort sums, word problems, spot Ahmed's mistake, traps |

### What it means

- **Three good lessons and two weak ones.** sun, rose and sand are close: no student put sun or rose last, and only one put sand last. sky and lime trail by a wide margin.
- **sun wins on preference.** It took 7 of 15 first places. The reason students gave is almost always the same: it felt like the real exam. Word problems, sorting which rule applies, and catching a mistake all teach *when* to use a rule, not just *how*.
- **rose wins on understanding.** It has the best clarity (8.8), the best predicted memory (8.07) and the best fun score. The staircase that turns into a rectangle is the single most praised moment in the study, the place where «÷ ٢» finally makes sense. It also has the most complaints about length (10).
- **sand is the safest.** It has the fewest would-quit moments (2), and both of those are about the trivial first rule, not about the method. Its tap-to-pair coloured arcs are the second most praised moment. It loses ground on its guess timers and on its claim of «قاعدتين في دقيقتين».
- **sky is the most polarising.** It got 2 first places but 9 last places. Its skip-if-you-know challenge delighted the impatient students. For the other students, the 10-second countdown on the very first screen, before anything had been taught, was the worst moment of the whole study.
- **lime is clear but dull.** Its clarity is fine (8.07), but it has the lowest fun and come-back scores and the most would-quit moments (12). Students called the naming sticker and the «ليش» screens filler, and the same rule-building tile puzzle came back 3–4 times.
- **Nothing makes students want to come back.** The best come-back average is 6.93. No lesson creates a strong pull to return tomorrow, so that has to come from the review loop and the game layer, not from the lesson itself.

## 2. Each lesson in detail

### sun — E «أيّ قاعدة؟» (1st: Borda 45, 7 first places)

**Why students liked it.** It felt like real Qudrat practice. They named the sort game «فرّزها», the word problems (chairs, savings, pages read, a pyramid of tins), «أحمد غلط — وين؟» and the «ولا وحدة» traps. The diagnostic feedback was praised by name: «نسيت ÷٢» and «نسيت + ١» say what went wrong instead of just showing the answer. The ١ + … + ١٠٠ opening hooked the short-video users. The main complaints were the streak wipe and the trap that was never taught.

**Where students would have quit (6).**
- **4: one miss wiped the in-lesson streak**, often on a trap they had not been taught (عبدالله, مازن, حمد, ليان).
- **2: the review repeated itself.** The same savings or pages problem came back with the same numbers (مازن, محمد).
- **1: patronising.** «٩ + ٩ + ٩ + ٩ + ٩ = ؟» came right after the screen had shown ٩ × ٥ = ٤٥ (دانة).

**Top wishes (15 students).**
| Wish | Count |
|---|---|
| Teach what to do when a sum doesn't start at 1, instead of «الحيلة ما تنفع» | **8** |
| Don't wipe the whole streak on one miss (a «درع» / shield) | **7** |
| More, and harder, exam-style word problems, with numbers that change | 4 |
| Review items that are really new, and a shorter review | 4 |
| A picture for *why* «÷ ٢» (rose's staircase), not a ready-made list | 3 |
| No timer on the opening guess, or more time | 2 |
| Cut the slow, obvious opening | 2 |

**A content bug that must be fixed.** sun marks sums like ٢ + ٣ + … + ١٢ or ٣ + ٤ + … + ٢٠ as «الحيلة ما تنفع». Mathematically that is wrong: pairing the ends still works, giving (first + last) × count ÷ 2. Nine students flagged it, and the strongest ones were the most confused. Separately, «صيد الغلط» items were often ambiguous: two identical tokens such as «٩ × ٩» or «٥ × ٥», or both numbers wrong in «١٩ × ٢٠ ÷ ٢». Eight students reported this.

**Quotes.**
- هيا: «لما طلعت لي «تفرّق بينهم — وهذا اللي يطيّح الناس في الاختبار» حسيت إني فعلاً أتجهز للاختبار»
- بندر: «لأنه الوحيد اللي علمني «متى» أستخدم كل قاعدة مو بس «كيف». الباقي يعلمك القاعدة وبس، هذا يعلمك ما تنغش فيها»
- حمد: «السلسلة صفّرت بسبب سؤال فخ ما شرح لي عنه قبل، وقال «الحيلة ما تنفع» بس ما قال لي طيب كيف أحلها»

### rose — B «من الحبّات للقاعدة» (2nd: Borda 41, 4 first places, best clarity and memory)

**Why students liked it.** The staircase. Nearly every student named it as rose's best moment: each tap tops up a column, the two staircases form a rectangle, «والأزرق نص المستطيل», and «÷ ٢» is suddenly obvious. Several said this was the first time they had *understood* the formula rather than memorised it («أحسن من الأستاذة»). Students also liked that the rule card carries its little picture, which works as a memory cue, and the review item «هذي صورته… وش قاعدته؟».

**Where students would have quit (6).**
- **4: slow tapping at the start.** Tapping the grid row by row to build «٦ + ٦ + ٦ + ٦» felt like «لعب أطفال ابتدائي», and so did tapping six columns for the staircase (إبراهيم, مازن, جنى, ريان).
- **2: length.** The staircase explanation needed a pause to think, and the review asked for a heavy ١ + … + ٤٠ calculation (هيا, رزان).

**Top wishes (15 students).**
| Wish | Count |
|---|---|
| On a miss, say what I forgot («نسيتي ÷٢») or replay the picture, and give a retry with new numbers | **6** |
| Shorter, and an honest time (it promised «٣ دقايق»; students measured 5½–7½) | **5** |
| Name the rule after its picture, e.g. «حيلة الدرج» (the staircase shows no "ends") | 4 |
| Let the picture build itself in a quick animation instead of tap-tap-tap | 3 |
| Cut the multiplication rule and open on the staircase | 3 |
| Leaderboard, or cards that level up like Clash | 3 |
| More word problems and a harder review (٤٥ + ٤٥ «مو حيلة أصلًا») | 3 |
| Let me choose which slot a number goes into (auto-fill put «١١» in the wrong blank) | 2 |

**Quotes.**
- وليد: «والله يا خوي درس الدرج والمربعات فهّمني ليش نقسم على ٢ بعد سنين وأنا صامّها»
- دانة: «أول مرة أفهم من قلب ليش ÷٢، وما عاد أحتاج أحفظ القانون»
- عبدالله: «ياليت يصير أقصر ويسمي القاعدة باسم يشبه الصورة (مثلًا «حيلة الدرج»)، وكان ودي لما أنسى ÷٢ يرجع لي صورة الدرج ويوريني إني أخذت المستطيل كله»

### sand — A «خمّن ثم اكتشف» (3rd: Borda 35, fewest quits)

**Why students liked it.** Tapping to pair the ends of ١ … ١٠ draws coloured arcs («١ + ١٠ = ١١»), and «طلّعتها بنفسك!» makes the student feel they found the trick themselves. Students described it as like a game, with no wall of text. The «٦ ورا بعض» counter felt like a streak to the gamers. Students who panic under time pressure ranked it highest overall, despite its guess timers.

**Where students would have quit (2).** Both were the first rule: «١٢ + ١٢ + ١٢ + ١٢ = ١٢ × ٤؟ يا رجال أنا ثالث ثانوي» (عبدالله, جنى). Nobody wanted to quit during the method itself.

**Top wishes (15 students).**
| Wish | Count |
|---|---|
| Fix the guess timer: longer, optional or gone, and no «تخمين حلو» or «خلص الوقت» next to «صح!» when nothing registered | **8** |
| Honest duration («قاعدتين في دقيقتين» beside a «٣ دقايق» chip, then ~6 min) | **6** |
| Skip or toughen the trivial first rule; make the guess something you can't already do | 5 |
| Bridge «٥ أزواج × ١١» to «١٠ × ١١ ÷ ٢» in one sentence (why ÷ ٢ = number of pairs) | 4 |
| One word problem, or one trap that doesn't start at 1 | 3 |

Smaller copy bugs: «اكتب القاعدة بكلماتك» is really a tile bank, and «نفس الحيلة — أسرع» introduced the *other* rule.

**Quotes.**
- ليان: «حسيت إني أنا اللي اكتشفتها، وبعدها «طلّعتها بنفسك!»»
- رزان: «الشاشة تقول «٥ أزواج × ١١» وتحتها المعادلة ١٠ × ١١ ÷ ٢، لخبطني شوي ليش مو ٥ × ١١ على طول»
- محمد: «فيه حماس sky وفيه وضوح rose، وما حسيت إنه درس»

### sky — C «أمثلة تتلاشى» (4th: Borda 17, 2 first places but 9 last places)

**Why students liked it.** The students who liked it were the strong and impatient ones: «حلها في ١٠ ثواني وتتخطّى الشرح» respects what they already know, and the «قفزة +٢٠» reward felt like a game (عبدالله, جنى, مازن, دانة, محمد). Some patient students praised the fading: a miss steps back one rung («٦٠ × __ ÷ ٢») instead of just marking it wrong («حسيته مدرّس صبور», هيا; also بندر and ريم).

**Where students would have quit (11).**
- **9: the 10-second challenge**, on the first screen or the second rule's. The countdown starts before the question can be read. ليان typed the right ٦٦ and still got «خلص الوقت». The second challenge asks for ١ + … + ٢٠ before the trick has been taught.
- **2: the slow worked example** forced on someone who timed out on something they knew (مازن, ريان).

**Top wishes (15 students).**
| Wish | Count |
|---|---|
| Show the pairs as a picture (sand's arcs or rose's staircase), not «كل زوج = ١١ والأزواج نص العدد» in text | **10** |
| Teach first; make the timed challenge optional, or put it at the end | **8** |
| More time, a second chance if only the clock beat me, or an «أعرفها» skip | 4 |
| Beat my own record, or see friends' times | 2 |

Integrity bugs: accuracy showed 100% after two timed-out challenges. «استخدمتها قبل ما تعرف اسمها» appeared after the student had been *shown* the worked example. A fast student who skips never sees *why* the trick works (جنى: «ياليت بعد ما أتخطى يعطيني صورة سريعة ثانيتين توضح ليش تشتغل»).

**Quotes.**
- إبراهيم: «خلص الوقت قبل لا أكتب وحسيت بفشل من أول ثانية، ولو أنا بالبيت كان سكرت»
- حمد: «اللي مثلي يتوتر من الوقت راح يقفل التطبيق من أول شاشة»
- جنى: «حليت في ٩ ثواني وتخطيت الشرح على طول، ما ضيع وقتي بأشياء ابتدائية»

### lime — D «اسمها وليش» (5th: Borda 12, no first places)

**Why students liked it.** The «ليش تشتغل؟» question earned «فاهمها مو حافظها!», and a few students loved it for that (وليد, رزان, أثير, حمد, ريم). The day 3 → 10 → 24 → 45 → exam timeline reassured three students that there is a plan (رزان, بندر, جنى). The phone-style notice «حيلة الطرفين تناديك» charmed two. A short name did stick for some.

**Where students would have quit (12).**
- **5: rebuilding the same rule from tiles** for the third or fourth time in review.
- **3: the sticker screens** «لها اسم — اضغط الاسم يلصق عليها» and «صار لها اسم» («ضغطة بس عشان ضغطة»).
- **3: the «ليش» questions felt like school.** One student skipped them at once, and one example answer read «لأن ٧ و ٤ قريبين من بعض» («سؤال لثالث ابتدائي»).
- **1: «أرقام جديدة» that weren't new.** The same ١ + … + ٤٠ came straight back.

**Top wishes (15 students).**
| Wish | Count |
|---|---|
| Fewer tile rebuilds; review with real problems instead | **6** |
| Truly new numbers when a missed item returns | 4 |
| Say what I got wrong (forgot ÷ ٢, used ٥٠ instead of ٥١), and a hint that helps | 3 |
| Cut the naming and «ليش» filler and get to solving | 3 |
| Skip the easy rule, harder items | 3 |
| A picture or something that moves | 2 |
| More excitement or a challenge | 2 |
| Don't print the rule's name above the question; it gives the answer away | 1 |

**Quotes.**
- مازن: «شاشتين بس عشان اسم، هنا كنت بسكّر»
- أثير: «صار شغل يدين مو تفكير»
- ليان: «كل سؤال مكتوب فوقه اسم القاعدة «احسبها باسمها» فما أفكر أي وحدة أستخدم، كأنه يغششني»

Two tensions to design around:
- **Wish 5 against the strong students.** The fix is not "no challenge" but "a challenge that cannot fail you": an opening item with no visible countdown. A fast right answer *earns* the skip, and a slow or wrong answer simply continues the lesson with no «خلص الوقت». Speed is reported afterwards («حليتها في ٦ ثواني»), never imposed.
- **Wish 1 against wish 4.** A visual that needs taps is slower. Students asked for the arcs to be tappable (sand: «حسيت إني أنا اللي اكتشفتها») but for rose's grid to build itself («بدل ما أضغط خمس مرات»). The rule: tap only where the tap *is* the discovery (pairing the ends). Animate anything else, at reel speed.

## 4. Recommended final design

**Build on sun's structure** (teach fast, then discriminate, then exam-style). **Teach the "why" with sand's arcs.** **Keep rose's staircase for the moment a student forgets ÷ ٢.** **Borrow sky's step-back on a miss.** **Keep lime's timeline and notice for the review.** Remove every countdown from learning, which Qudrati already requires: «No timer while learning».

Target: two rules in **about 3 real minutes, 11 screens** on an all-right run. Measure it with a stopwatch before printing any duration.

| # | Screen | From | What it does, and what changes from the prototype |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | **Start** | sun / sand | «قاعدتين — وتعرف متى تستخدم كل وحدة». Show the time only once it is measured. lime had no start screen and two students missed one. |
| 1 | **Rule 1 in one tap** | sun + sky's skip | «٨ + ٨ + ٨ + ٨ + ٨ — كم مرة تكرر الـ٨؟», three choices. Right → the terms collapse into ٨ × ٥ = ٤٠ and the yellow rule card «العدد × مرّاته» lands with its blue term chips. No grid tapping, no worked example, no "use it at once" on the same numbers (دانة: «يستهبلون علي»). |
| 1b | *(only on a miss)* | rose | The block grid **builds itself** in about 2 s («٨ فوق، ٥ مرات جنب»), then the same question with new numbers. |
| 2 | **Rule 2 hook** | sun | «١ + ٢ + … + ١٠٠ = ؟» with three choices and **no draining bar**. Any answer → «٥٠٥٠ — بثانيتين؟ فيه حيلة». Nothing is marked wrong. Never «خلص الوقت», never praise for a tap that didn't register. |
| 3 | **Pair the ends** | sand | The student taps ١ … ١٠ to pair them; each tap draws a coloured arc «١ + ١٠ = ١١». Five taps, because here the tap *is* the discovery. |
| 4 | **The ÷ ٢ bridge** | sand, fixed | One animated line, the gap three students fell into: «١٠ أعداد ← ٥ أزواج (١٠ ÷ ٢) ← ٥ × ١١ = ١٠ × ١١ ÷ ٢». Then the rule card «حيلة الطرفين». The name now matches the arcs. |
| 5 | **Use it once** | sun | «١ + … + ٢٠ = ؟» as choices whose distractors are the real mistakes. The sheet names the slip: «نسيت + ١» / «نسيت ÷ ٢». **On «نسيت ÷ ٢»:** rose's staircase animates in about 3 s («أخذت المستطيل كله — والمطلوب نصّه»), then sky's step-back «٢٠ × __ ÷ ٢», then a new sum. This is عبدالله's wish word for word. |
| 6 | **Build both rules once** | sun «كمّل القاعدتين» / sand | One screen: finish both rules from tiles that mix pieces of both. This is the *only* tile build in the lesson (lime showed what three or four do). A miss: the wrong tiles shake back, and the right ones stay. |
| 7 | **«فرّزها»** | sun | Four sums tapped into the two rule buttons, with look-alikes back to back («٤ + ٤ + ٤ + ٤» against «١ + ٢ + ٣ + ٤»). A wrong tap gets a one-line reason. «ولا وحدة» is used **only for sums that truly fit neither**, such as «٧ + ٧ + ٨ + ٧». A sum that doesn't start at 1 is never called «ما تنفع». |
| 8 | **Word problem** | sun | The pages or pyramid-of-tins problem: pick the rule, then the answer. One problem is enough. |
| 9 | **«أحمد غلط — وين؟»** | sun, fixed | Exactly **one** wrong token and no duplicate tokens (e.g. «١ + … + ٢٠ = ٢٠ × ٢٠ ÷ ٢», tap the second ٢٠ → «٢١ مو ٢٠»). |
| 10 | **Comeback** | all | Anything missed returns once, with **genuinely new numbers** (the lime, rose and sun prototypes all repeated items). |
| 11 | **End** | app's win screen | XP, accuracy and time. Timeouts count, so no fake 100%. A run counter that a single slip *dents* but does not zero (a one-miss shield). «حليتها في ٦ ثواني — اكسر رقمك بكرة» is the only place speed appears. |

**Cut from the prototypes:** the sticker naming screens (lime), the «ليش» multiple choice as a gate (offer it as a small optional «ليش؟» link on the rule card, with no star penalty), the opening 10-second challenge (sky), the guess timers (sand, sun), tapping rows into the grid (rose), and repeated tile rebuilds (lime, sand review).

**Teach the "not from 1" case before it is ever used as a trap.** This is a content decision for you. Either add one optional "stretch" item after screen 7, «٣ + ٤ + … + ٢٠؟ ← (أول + آخر) × عددهم ÷ ٢» (or «١ إلى ٢٠ ناقص ٣»), or leave such sums out of this lesson entirely. Do not mark them «ما تنفع»: that is wrong, and it was the study's most-reported problem.

**What this means for the app as it stands.** Qudrati's short lessons follow a similar arc («اكتشف ← جرّب ← ثبّت ← تحدَّ»). The «عبّ القاعدة» / «الحين بالأرقام» screens currently follow every rule in node 1's first seven lessons. The study supports *one* rule build per rule (or one combined build per lesson) and argues against stacking more builds in review. lime's students called that «شغل يدين مو تفكير». The study's strongest new pieces for the app are:
- the discrimination round («فرّزها»);
- diagnostic wrong-answer sheets («نسيت ÷ ٢»);
- the picture replay on a miss;
- removing every countdown from first exposure.

### How the 90-day review should feel

What students asked for, mapped onto the schedule in `research/rule-memory.md` (intervals of about 1 → 3 → 7 → 15 → 21 days, capped before the exam):

- **Tiny and honest.** 30–60 seconds, 1–3 items, a promise kept («دقيقة وحدة» must take a minute). Thirteen students asked for this, in almost these words.
- **Problems, not drills.** Mostly "apply with fresh numbers" and "which rule?" (the interleaving item students loved in sun), plus an occasional «أحمد غلط». A tile rebuild of the rule at most once every few reviews, never two in a row.
- **Really new every time.** Generators, never a repeated stem. Students noticed every repeat and called it «غش».
- **Cued by the picture.** rose's «هذي صورته… وش قاعدته؟» was a favourite review item. The rule card's picture is a strong retrieval cue.
- **A miss teaches.** First the step-back (sky), then the named slip and the picture replay, and the item returns tomorrow. No red wall, and no streak wipe.
- **A visible plan.** lime's timeline «يوم ٣ ← ١٠ ← ٢٤ ← ٤٥ ← الاختبار» and the memory bar draining over «بعد ٣ أيام…» reassured students. The plan could live on the due card, counted down to `S.exam`.
- **A gentle call.** «حيلة الطرفين تناديك — دقيقة وحدة» charmed students. As a home-screen due card (and a notification on Android), it is the Duolingo-style pull back that no lesson provided by itself.
- **Speed comes last, and is optional.** Once a rule is well held (`h > 14` in rule-memory.md), offer «اكسر رقمك»: the student's own record, and friends' times if a social layer ever exists. Strong students asked for this, and anxious students asked for it never to be compulsory.

## 5. Caveats

- **Simulated students.** All 15 are AI personas, probably from the same model family that built the prototypes. They write articulately, they share blind spots, and they may reward what a language model finds "exam-like" or "clever". Treat the ranking as a strong hypothesis to test with a handful of real Saudi students, not a measurement.
- **No sound.** They played through a scripted phone with no audio. Sound and haptics, which carry much of a Duolingo-style lesson's feel, were not judged at all.
- **No real delay.** «بعد ٣ أيام…» was a simulated screen played seconds later. "Remember in 3 months" is each persona's *prediction*, not a retention result. Nothing here measures whether a rule survives 90 days.
- **Order effects.** The order was shuffled per student. The average Borda points by play position were 1.73, 2.40, 1.47, 1.93 and 2.47 (1st to 5th played; neutral is 2.0). Most of that spread is explained by which lessons happened to land in each slot (for example, sky was played first four times). There is a mild leftover boost for the lesson played last (+3.5 points over the expected 33.5). Carry-over was real in the comments: students judged later lessons against visuals from earlier ones («لو ما شفتها قبل [في sand] يمكن ما فهمت»), and the first rule felt staler with each repetition.
- **Tool artefacts.** Many timer failures came partly from the slow scripted input. A student's tap arrived after the clock had run out, so the timer complaints are inflated. The design lesson still holds, since real weak students are slow too. The 5–7½ minute durations students reported include tool latency; the QA passes estimated about 2.7–3.5 minutes for a human. Hidden leftover DOM text («كفو!») and chip mis-taps from text matching are probably tooling issues, not things a real student would see.
- **Implementations, not pure methods.** Each lesson was built by a different designer with different polish and bugs: sun's wrong «ما تنفع», rose's auto-filling slots, lime's repeated "new" numbers, sky's fake 100%. The ranking compares these five builds. A fixed sky or lime might score differently.
- **The trivial first rule skews the result.** Every lesson had to teach «العدد × مرّاته», which most personas already knew. Lessons that spent more screens on it (rose's grid, lime's naming, sky's worked example) were penalised for it.
- **Small segments.** The splits rest on 2–7 students each. The two "Duolingo users" are contradictory profiles. Every student finished every lesson, so "would quit" is a stated intention, not real drop-off.
