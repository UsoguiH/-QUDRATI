4 · Vector face rig

The drawing was flat vector art before it was flattened, so the parts that need to move trace straight back to paths. tools/vectorize_face.py pulls out the brows, eyelids, eye whites, pupils and mouth as 13 SVG paths — 12 KB — and erases them from the raster underneath. Everything that never moves (hair, beard, glasses, nose, body) stays raster, because re-tracing it would only add error.

Live rig

Breathing, blinking, looking around and talking — all from one still drawing.

Drive it

Presets set brows, lids and mouth together. Toggles run underneath, always.

Why it reads as alive. Blinks are irregular — 2.4–6.5 s apart, and about one in five is a double. Closing takes 70 ms, opening 150 ms, because a real lid falls and is lifted. The pupils dart to a new spot in 45 ms and then hold; eyes never drift smoothly without something to follow. A metronome blink and a gliding pupil are the two things that read instantly as fake.
Note. The four swap images are the old flat placeholder states, so they jump in scale — that is the art, not the animation. Real states cut from one approved reference will land in register.
