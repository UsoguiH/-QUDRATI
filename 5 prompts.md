## After each image lands

1. Run it through `tools/slice_mascot.py` to cut the cream background to alpha. The
   background can never be black: his hair, shoes, stick and book outline are all
   near-black, and the slicer flood-fills from the border.
2. Flatten to black at 96px. It must still read as this character.
3. Hair volume, glasses shape, the silver streak, vest colour and the book must match
   the sheet exactly. The cover must read قدرات, spelled correctly, not mirrored, not
   duplicated. Arabic text is the most common image-model failure, check it every time.
   Drift means the anchor did not hold, regenerate.
4. PNG, transparent, about 512px tall, at most 45 KB, committed pre-optimised. No
   build step exists.
5. Add the key to `MASCOT_STATES` in `js/app.js` **the same day** the PNG is committed,
   never before. An unlisted state renders nothing, so a missing file cannot 404 the
   live site.
6. Never mirror him with CSS. `scaleX(-1)` reverses the book's text. He points to the
   viewer's left, so he sits at the right edge of an RTL column.
