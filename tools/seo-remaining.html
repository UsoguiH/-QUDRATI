Decision for you Where I stopped

Your idea works: the «قدراتي» loading screen can be what Google measures. Do it?

A correction first. The 6.6 s and 15 s I gave you earlier were Lighthouse's simulated slow phone. I measured again on a real throttled phone (same slow 4G, CPU 4× slower). That's closer to what Google's ranking data uses, which comes from real visitors' phones. The home page is 3.2 s live, not 6.6 s, and nowhere near 15 s.

Google measures speed by when the biggest text or picture on screen appears (LCP). Target: under 2.5 s.

Your loading screen's «قدراتي» is the first and biggest thing on screen. But it's drawn as shapes (SVG paths), and Google doesn't count shapes, only text and images. So Google skips your name and waits for a later line of text.

Lighthouse with real throttling, out of 4.5 s. "On my PC" runs are slower overall than live, because my local server doesn't compress files like Vercel does. Compare the red part, not the totals. With the name counted, the red part is gone: Google's measurement lands on the very first paint. On live, first paint is about 2.1–2.3 s, so that would be under 2.5 s.

How: a picture of the name's letters, identical to the drawn name, sits behind it in the loading screen. Google counts the picture, and you see exactly the same screen. Once the hand-off starts I hide the picture, so the flying name and the dots stay as they are. I tested it on a temporary copy of the page (now deleted). Google picked the name as the main content in every run.
